BuddyPoint AO3 Bridge

Sign in here, then use the AO3 menu on your reader to browse bookmarks and download fics.

Get the token with docker compose exec bridge python bridge.py token. Enter the same token in the reader’s Bridge settings.

Enter your device token to get started.

Original EPUBs are kept alongside optimised copies. Your AO3 password is used for sign-in and is not saved. The bridge stores the resulting session in its data volume.
